About these figures & data connections

Charts count unique coverage items, not serial numbers, page numbers or estimated reach. Linked clipping evidence is counted with its article once. Unlinked clippings and e-paper issues remain separate items. Mixed-language labels are retained as categories. A blank date is never replaced with the date the item was found.

Publication-month filters include whole months. Year-only records appear in the yearly chart; month and weekday charts explicitly report what they exclude. Source links lead to the publisher when available. This is a read-only dashboard; it does not change the archive.

    Checks for new data every five minutes while this tab is visible. A refresh checks the saved archive; it does not crawl the internet for new articles.
